Validation roadmap

These are targets and measurement plans, not current traction claims.

Stage 1

Real-project validation

Measure manual analysis time versus VITA analysis time, outputs, differences, missing data and investment decisions across real projects.

Stage 2

Commercial validation

Convert validated workflows into paid VITA engagements and measure repeatability, customer value and a sustainable revenue model.

Stage 3

Investor evidence

Publish appropriate aggregate KPIs, prepare a data room and document product, market, IP and financial evidence before a larger financing process.

What is deliberately not claimed

No invented traction.
We do not publish customer counts, revenue, pilot numbers, accuracy percentages, time savings or investment commitments unless they are supported by documented evidence. Future targets such as 10 real projects, 3–5 paying customers and a €1–2M financing target remain roadmap objectives until achieved.
No research-to-product shortcut.
Oxygen ClimateTech, SOLIS-LUMEN, EDCS and ANNE research are kept separate from commercial product claims and should be evaluated according to their own maturity and evidence.
