VITA Energy Intelligence

Energy data → intelligence → verified investment decision. VITA is Vitavolt Global's product direction for turning real industrial energy data into technical, financial, carbon and scenario-based investment analysis.

Investment thesis

Industrial energy investments increasingly require one decision framework across electricity consumption, tariffs, renewable generation, storage, carbon exposure and financial return. VITA is being developed to connect those inputs and translate them into an investment decision workflow.

DATA→VITA ENGINE→FINANCE→CARBON→SCENARIO→DECISION
VITA is presented as a developing technology/product platform. This page does not claim market scale, revenue, certification or performance beyond publicly documented evidence.

What VITA analyzes

Energy & infrastructure

Electricity consumption, tariff structure, roof/land conditions, GES generation, BESS and production assumptions.

Technical outputs

Potential PV capacity, production, self-consumption and storage-related scenarios.

Financial intelligence

CAPEX/OPEX, savings, payback, NPV, IRR and project economics.

Carbon intelligence

CO₂ and carbon-cost considerations integrated into investment scenarios.

AI verification layer

VITA is designed to work with ANNE AI as a verification and reasoning layer. The intended workflow is not “AI replaces engineering”; it is “VITA calculates → ANNE checks assumptions, missing data, sensitivity and confidence.”

Data Quality

Identify missing or inconsistent inputs before a decision is presented.

Technical reasoning

Review GES/BESS assumptions and scenario consistency.

Financial reasoning

Challenge economic assumptions and sensitivity before investment decisions.

Report intelligence

Turn the validated analysis into a decision-oriented report.
