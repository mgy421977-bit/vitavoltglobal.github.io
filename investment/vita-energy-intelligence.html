VITA Energy Intelligence

Energy data → intelligence → verified investment decision. VITA is Vitavolt Global's product direction for turning real industrial energy data into technical, financial, carbon and scenario-based investment analysis.

Investment thesis

Industrial energy investments require a common framework across consumption, tariffs, renewable generation, storage, carbon exposure and financial return. VITA is being developed to connect these inputs into an investment decision workflow.

DATA→VITA ENGINE→FINANCE→CARBON→SCENARIO→DECISION

What VITA analyzes

Energy & infrastructure

Electricity consumption, tariffs, roof/land conditions, GES generation, BESS and production assumptions.

Technical outputs

PV capacity, production, self-consumption and storage scenarios.

Financial intelligence

CAPEX/OPEX, savings, payback, NPV, IRR and project economics.

Carbon intelligence

CO₂ and carbon-cost considerations integrated into investment scenarios.

VITA + ANNE verification layer

VITA calculates; ANNE checks assumptions, missing data, sensitivity and confidence. This is a verification and reasoning direction, not a claim that AI replaces engineering.

Data quality

Identify missing or inconsistent inputs.

Technical reasoning

Review GES/BESS assumptions and scenario consistency.

Financial reasoning

Challenge economic assumptions before decisions.
