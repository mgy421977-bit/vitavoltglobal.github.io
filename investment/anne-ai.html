ANNE AI

Open cognitive architecture research for verified, controlled and explainable AI workflows. ANNE is Vitavolt Research's AI research direction, connected to the VITA Energy Intelligence ecosystem and designed around evidence, verification, provenance and controlled agency.

Strategic thesis

Modern AI systems can produce useful outputs while still requiring stronger controls around assumptions, provenance, verification, memory and action. ANNE explores an open cognitive architecture in which heterogeneous AI capabilities can be orchestrated and their outputs examined before they influence a decision or workflow.

AI CAPABILITIES→ORCHESTRATION→REASONING→VERIFICATION→CONTROLLED ACTION
ANNE is an AGI-oriented research direction, not a claim of completed AGI or autonomous general intelligence. Investment and partnership discussions should be evaluated against the latest public technical evidence.

Research capabilities

Heterogeneous AI orchestration

Coordinate specialized AI capabilities rather than assuming one model should perform every task.

Neuro-symbolic reasoning

Explore combinations of learned AI capabilities with structured reasoning and explicit constraints.

Cognitive memory

Research memory structures that can support continuity and context while keeping provenance visible.

Verification & provenance

Trace assumptions, evidence and intermediate reasoning so outputs can be reviewed rather than blindly accepted.

Controlled agency

Explore bounded action and decision workflows with explicit controls instead of unrestricted autonomy.

VITA + ANNE

The commercial and research paths are connected without being conflated. VITA is the energy intelligence product direction; ANNE provides a verification and reasoning layer for the analysis workflow.

Industrial Energy Data→VITA→ANNE→Verified Analysis→Decision

This architecture creates a potential bridge between energy infrastructure, AI research and decision intelligence while preserving a clear distinction between product capabilities and research hypotheses.
