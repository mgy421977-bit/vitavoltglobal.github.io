ClimateTech Research Vision · Season 1

Oxygen ClimateTech

Integrated Carbon, Energy, Biology, Water, Biomass & Digital Intelligence. A research and development vision exploring how technologies from different disciplines can work together as interconnected systems.

🌍 We have a dream: to design a more livable world.
Research status: Oxygen ClimateTech is a research vision, not a claim that every component is commercially proven or production-ready. We distinguish established technologies, technologies under development and research hypotheses, and we aim to validate assumptions with measurement, engineering analysis and lifecycle assessment.

The system we envision

Rather than treating decarbonization, energy, water, biology and digital intelligence as isolated domains, we explore their interfaces.

Research components

Carbon Management

Industrial CO₂ management, emissions reduction pathways and integration with biological and energy systems.

Photosynthetic Systems

Microalgae, bioreactors and other photosynthetic organisms as research pathways for carbon–oxygen and biomass cycles.

Miscanthus & Carbon Fields

Research into fast-growing C4 plants, biomass production, sustainable land use and carbon management. Quantification requires site-specific measurement and lifecycle analysis.

Biom

A systems concept for biological life, resource cycles and controlled environmental processes.

Oxygen Factory

An industrial research concept connecting carbon, biological processes, energy and resource recovery.

Renewable Energy + BESS

Solar generation, storage and energy management as an energy backbone for integrated systems.

Water & Resource Recovery

Water reuse, heat recovery and circular resource flows as supporting infrastructure.

VITA + ANNE

Energy and carbon data analysis, scenario comparison, verification, uncertainty identification and decision support.

What we are trying to learn

What is proven?

Separate commercially established technologies from experimental components.

What can we measure?

Define energy, carbon, water, biomass and process metrics before making performance claims.

What must be validated?

Test system interactions, lifecycle impacts, economics, safety and scalability.
