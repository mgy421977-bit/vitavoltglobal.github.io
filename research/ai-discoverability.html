Research project

ANNE AI — Adaptive Neural Nexus Engine

AGI-oriented open cognitive architecture research. The public research scope includes cognitive orchestration, heterogeneous AI coordination, semantic validation, memory, provenance, deterministic verification and controlled agency. ANNE does not claim achieved AGI.

Research project

EDCS — Emergent Drone Coordination System

Research into distributed and self-healing drone swarm coordination using local decision-making. The current public implementation is a simulation prototype; hardware prototyping, UWB integration and field trials are planned.

PV R&D

SOLIS-LUMEN

Research into micro-optical light management and passive thermal architecture for next-generation photovoltaic module design. Modelling and prototype testing are used to evaluate the concept; performance targets are not guarantees.
