What Is ANNE AI?

ANNE AI nedir?

ANNE (Adaptive Neural Nexus Engine) is an AGI-oriented open cognitive architecture research project. It investigates whether an explicit cognitive orchestration layer can make model-assisted reasoning more reliable, traceable, controllable and extensible.

ANNE (Adaptive Neural Nexus Engine), AGI odaklı açık bir bilişsel mimari araştırma projesidir.

Repository roles: anne-ai = active working surface · anne-core = cognitive kernel · anne = frozen main-line (not the active target).Repo rolleri: anne-ai = çalışan yüzey · anne-core = çekirdek · anne = dondurulmuş ana hat.
Research boundary: ANNE does not claim that completed AGI has been achieved. AGI is treated as an architectural research direction.Araştırma sınırı: ANNE, tamamlanmış AGI iddiasında bulunmaz.
