Vitavolt Research · ANNE AI

What Is ANNE AI?

ANNE (Adaptive Neural Nexus Engine) is an AGI-oriented open cognitive architecture research project. It investigates whether an explicit cognitive orchestration layer can make model-assisted reasoning more reliable, traceable, controllable and extensible.

Research boundary: ANNE does not claim that completed Artificial General Intelligence has been achieved. AGI is treated as an architectural research direction.

The research question

ANNE studies a systems-level question: can heterogeneous AI models and tools be coordinated through explicit decomposition, evaluation, memory, verification, executive synthesis and controlled agency rather than relying on a single model alone?

What the architecture explores

  • Cognitive orchestration and task decomposition
  • Heterogeneous AI/model coordination
  • Semantic validation and contradiction handling
  • Cognitive and episodic memory
  • Neuro-symbolic reasoning
  • Evidence and provenance tracking
  • Deterministic verification
  • Bounded planning and metacognition
  • Agency and safety gates
  • Controlled tool execution

Evidence discipline

ANNE separates IMPLEMENTED, EXPERIMENTAL, HYPOTHESIS and ROADMAP. A benchmark, successful response or mathematical derivation is not treated by itself as evidence of general intelligence, consciousness or autonomous safety.
