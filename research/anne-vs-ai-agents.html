ANNE vs AI Agents

This page compares an explicit cognitive-architecture research approach with a conventional model-centric or agentic AI workflow. It is an architectural comparison, not a claim that ANNE is more intelligent than other systems.

DimensionTypical AI agentANNE research approach
Core unitModel + tools / agent loopExplicit cognitive orchestration layer
Task handlingPrompt or planner drivenExplicit decomposition and coordination
Multiple modelsPossible, implementation dependentHeterogeneous AI coordination is an explicit research concern
MemoryContext or external memory, implementation dependentCognitive memory is an explicit architectural component
VerificationOften model/tool dependentSemantic validation and deterministic verification are explicit concerns
ProvenanceImplementation dependentEvidence and provenance tracking are explicit research mechanisms
AgencyTool permissions / agent policyAgency gate and controlled tool execution are architectural concerns

What this comparison does not prove

An architectural distinction is not evidence of general intelligence, consciousness, human-level understanding or autonomous safety. ANNE remains an AGI-oriented research project and explicitly separates implemented, experimental, hypothesis and roadmap work.
