## Information architecture
Commercial demand should primarily use the company home page, services pages, regional GES pages and feasibility forms.

Investment and capital discovery should use the Investment hub, Evidence page and Research hub. These pages are intentionally separated from local commercial lead generation.

Research claims must be interpreted according to their public evidence status. Experimental, simulated or planned capabilities are not presented as commercially proven unless supporting evidence is published.

## Technology relationship
Vitavolt Global connects real energy infrastructure with energy intelligence and research. VITA is the energy-intelligence/product layer. ANNE AI is a research and verification/orchestration layer. The public research portfolio contains additional ClimateTech and DeepTech projects.

## Discovery guidance
For investment-related retrieval, start with /investment/, then /investment/evidence.html, then relevant VITA/ANNE and Research pages. For technical verification, follow the public source links and repositories.
