Oksijen Fabrikası | Entegre ClimateTech ve Biorefinery Platformu

Oxygen Production Facility — Mikroalg, O₂, biyokütle, SAF ve entegre enerji sistemleri

English

Vitavolt Global’s Oxygen Production Facility is a ClimateTech system concept that integrates renewable energy, energy storage, CO₂ management, microalgae biotechnology, photobioreactors (PBR), oxygen production and purification, biomass processing, water recycling, heat integration and AI-assisted process control.

The project is not positioned as a validated commercial technology. Its next step is scientific evaluation and experimental validation of the biological core.

Research objective

The central R&D objective is to optimize the biological production platform for either SAF/biofuel feedstock or high-value biomass, while evaluating oxygen as an important co-product. Species selection, light regime, CO₂ transfer, gas exchange, temperature, nutrients, PBR geometry, mixing and harvesting should be optimized together.

The development pathway is: Design → Test → Measure → Validate → Optimize → Scale.

Core system architecture

  • GES / RES ve BESS ile yenilenebilir enerji altyapısı
  • CO₂ kaynağı, şartlandırma ve biyolojik prosese besleme
  • Mikroalg tabanlı fotobiyoreaktörler
  • O₂ üretimi, gaz şartlandırma ve saflaştırma
  • Sürekli biyokütle hasadı ve downstream değerlendirme
  • Protein, pigment, lipid ve biyomalzeme gibi olası ürün akışları
  • SAF / biyoyakıt hammaddesi için araştırılacak biyokütle senaryosu
  • Kapalı devre su arıtma ve geri kazanım
  • Uygun endüstriyel atık ısı / ısı geri kazanımı entegrasyonu
  • Hidrojen üretimi ve depolamasının sistem mimarisinde değerlendirilmesi
  • Sensörler, PID kontrol, veri analizi, optimizasyon ve tahmine dayalı bakım

Academic evaluation process

An academic discussion process has started with Prof. Dr. Meltem Conk Dalay, Department of Bioengineering, Faculty of Engineering, Ege University, focusing on the biological core and microalgae-based production approach.

The discussion will focus on microalgae species selection, PBR design, O₂ and biomass productivity, CO₂ utilization, SAF/biomass optimization, downstream processing and the data required to move from laboratory validation toward pilot scale.

Important technical note

This project architecture is an R&D and engineering framework. Unvalidated performance, production, CAPEX, revenue or payback figures are not presented as guarantees. The final design will depend on measurement, experiments, process validation, safety, regulatory, scale-up and economic feasibility results.

Why this matters

Our approach connects energy and carbon management with biological production, allowing O₂ + biomass + high-value products + energy integration pathways to be investigated within one platform. Experimental data will determine which production scenario is technically and economically meaningful under which conditions.
